# Third-party / Brand Assets

- The image files in `assets/brand/` and `public/brand/` originate from the logo uploaded by the project owner. The original is preserved as `ad-me-logo.png`; extra variants are **transparent-margin crops only**, with no recoloring or redrawing.
- The MIT license for the harness does not automatically license third-party trademarks, uploaded logos, third-party photos, fonts, or datasets. Obtain usage permission before publicly deploying/redistributing where necessary.
- The bundled `fixtures` contain synthetic fictional accounts and no real creator photos or external thumbnails.
