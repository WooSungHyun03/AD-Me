- `public/brand/figma/` came from the Figma design import (2026-11-24). `logo.png` is the Figma source logo. `hero.png`, `editorial.png` and `creator-editorial.png` are people imagery whose source, copyright and likeness consent are unrecorded: rights unverified, a release blocker until documented or replaced. See `docs/licenses/README.md`.
